Mac Sensors for Stream Deck

Temperatures, fan speeds, power, CPU usage, memory, storage and network rates from your Mac, on your Stream Deck keys and dials. Mac Sensors only reads; it changes no settings and controls no fans.

Get started → Install →

macOS 13.5 or later · Stream Deck app 7.1 or later and its Node 24 runtime · Tahoma Bold, which ships with macOS. No separate sensor app. Requirements and installation

Version 1.0.1.0 is on GitHub, where downloads need repository access. It is not on the Elgato Marketplace yet.

What goes on the deck

  • Sensor Reading →

    One to four readings on a key, with a sparkline, bar or ring under a single reading. A press cycles current, min, max and average.

    Where it goes: Any display key (Stream Deck models)

  • Sensor Dial →

    One reading, two rows or three rows on the touch strip. Turn through a reading set; local session min, max and average.

    Where it goes: A dial on a Stream Deck + or + XL

  • Sensor details →

    A Press choice on Sensor Reading: a page of tiles for the reading’s group, a filter or your own list, with a Back tile.

    Where it goes: Decks with a bundled detail view (Sensor details support)

  • Sensor Control →

    Steers Sensor Dials: change the reading, show a statistic, pause the auto cycle, pin, or reset statistics.

    Where it goes: Any key, pedal or Multi Action step

Which readings appear depends on the Mac and its macOS version. The measured M5 Pro reports 52: 14 from system counters and 38 from the SMC (Readings measured on the M5 Pro). CPU and GPU clock speeds and GPU usage are not offered.

What it does not do

Mac Sensors reads. It has no fan control and cannot write a sensor register. It installs no privileged helper, daemon or kernel extension and needs no administrator password. It makes no internet requests and sends no telemetry. You choose when to copy and share a support report. Privacy has the details.
