Sensor Reading

A reading key can show one large value, two stacked readings, three compact rows, or four color-coded cells. Labels and numbers fit measured Tahoma ink bounds.

Production-rendered sample data. The same Mac catalog labels appear in each layout.

Choose each reading separately. A custom label changes its presentation, not its identity. Unknown settings from a newer version remain in the saved document.

The normal press cycles the key’s selected statistic. Mac provider history is unavailable, so MIN, MAX and AVG show N/A instead of substituting the current value. Dial session statistics are a separate local feature.

One-reading keys can show a sparkline, bar or ring. A sparkline records genuine accepted observations while the action is visible. It is not a stored long-term history. Percentages use their fixed domain. Other bar and ring bounds follow observed values and include the configured threshold zones.

Warning and critical colors take priority over theme and custom text colors. Temperature conversion affects the displayed unit and threshold interpretation together.
